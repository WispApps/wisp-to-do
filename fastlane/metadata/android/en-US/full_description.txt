Wisp To Do is a private, offline-first task organizer.

• Create projects, lists and tasks.
• Set date and time reminders.
• Mark tasks as favorites and search lists and tasks.
• Export and import AES-256-GCM encrypted archives protected by your recovery phrase.
• Upload and restore encrypted backups using a Nextcloud WebDAV server you control.
• Choose a light or dark theme and one of ten interface languages.

Your tasks are stored locally unless you explicitly export them or use the Nextcloud backup. Cloud transfers contain encrypted backup data.
